# Design Craft & Interaction Principles

1. **Fluid Transitions**: Keep UI transitions fast (150ms-200ms) with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
2. **Visual Hierarchy**:
   - Eyebrows & Overlines: 11px uppercase, font-mono or font-medium, tracked out.
   - Values & Metrics: 24px-36px tabular numbers, high contrast.
   - Labels & Captions: 12px-13px neutral/secondary.
3. **Card Surfaces**:
   - Flat by default with 1px structural border.
   - 1px translation on hover without jarring size jumps.
4. **Data Density with Breathing Room**:
   - Compact table and list structures.
   - Avoid clutter: provide clear filters and distinct category tabs for multi-sport views.
