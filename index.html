Terms of Service

1. Service Scope

Goalie provides physiological data modeling and training load calculations for endurance athletics. All workout plans and pace forecasts are mathematical estimates.

2. User Responsibility

Athletes remain fully responsible for listening to their biological signals, managing injury risks, and consulting qualified physicians before engaging in maximal exertion.

1. Personal Data Protection (GDPR & UU PDP)

Biometric telemetry (heart rate time-series, GPS coordinates, VO2max estimates) is classified as Sensitive Personal Data and secured via Row-Level Security (RLS) isolation.

2. Zero Data Brokering

Your physiological records are strictly processed for your AI Coach context. We do not sell or monetize personal athlete data to third-party ad networks.
